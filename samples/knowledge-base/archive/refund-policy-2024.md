# Refund policy

Effective 1 January 2024.

## Annual subscriptions

A new annual subscription can be cancelled with a full refund within 14 days of the purchase
date. After 14 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable.

## Monthly subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions cancelled within 7 days of a renewal are refunded in proportion to the
unused days of that month. After 7 days, the month is not refunded.

## How to request a refund

Send an email to billing@tessera.example with the invoice number. Refunds are processed within
30 days.
