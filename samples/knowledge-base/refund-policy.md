# Refund policy

## Annual Pro subscriptions

A new annual Pro subscription can be cancelled with a full refund within 30 days of the
purchase date. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are not refundable, but the workspace
stays on Pro until the end of the paid year.

## Monthly Pro subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are not refunded. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the workspace
keeps Pro until the end of the month already paid.

## Enterprise

Refunds and credits for Enterprise customers follow their contract. Uptime-related service
credits are described in the SLA article.

## How to request a refund

The workspace owner opens a ticket with the category Billing, stating the invoice number.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 business days of approval.
