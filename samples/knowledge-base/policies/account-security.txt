ACCOUNT SECURITY

Two-factor authentication
Every agent can turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app. Workspace owners
on Pro and Enterprise can make it mandatory for all agents in the workspace settings.

Single sign-on
Enterprise workspaces can sign in with SAML single sign-on (for example Microsoft Entra ID or
Okta). When single sign-on is enforced, password login is disabled for every agent except the
workspace owner, who keeps a recovery login.

Password reset
Agents reset their own password from the login page; the link in the email expires after
60 minutes. Tessera staff never ask for a password, by email, chat or phone.

Locked accounts
After 10 failed login attempts in 15 minutes the account is locked for 30 minutes. A
workspace owner can unlock it earlier from the agent list.

Reporting a vulnerability
Security problems are reported to the security team through the form on the trust page, not
through a support ticket.
