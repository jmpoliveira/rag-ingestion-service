# Data retention and privacy

## Where data is stored

Workspaces are hosted in the European Union by default (Frankfurt and Dublin). Enterprise
customers can require that all data, including backups, stays in the EU.

## Retention

Ticket history is kept for 30 days on Free and 2 years on Pro. Enterprise customers choose a
retention period between 1 and 10 years. Attachments follow the same retention as their
ticket. Backups are kept for 35 days and then overwritten.

## Deleting data

The workspace owner can delete a single ticket at any time. A request to delete all personal
data about one end customer (a right-to-erasure request under the GDPR) is completed within
30 days, including backups as they expire.

When a workspace is cancelled, its data is kept for 60 days so that it can be reactivated,
and then permanently deleted.

## Sub-processors

The list of sub-processors (hosting, email delivery, payments) is published on the trust
page. Customers are notified 30 days before a new sub-processor is added.
