# Plans and pricing

Tessera Support Cloud is sold in three plans. Prices are per agent seat.

## Free

For teams trying the product. Up to 3 agent seats, email channel only, 1,000 tickets per
month and 30 days of ticket history. Support is community-only: there is no response-time
commitment for Free workspaces.

## Pro

For growing support teams. 19 EUR per seat per month on the monthly plan, or 15 EUR per seat
per month when billed annually. Includes email, chat and web forms, unlimited tickets,
automations, and 2 years of ticket history. Pro customers get email support from our team
during business hours.

## Enterprise

For organisations with compliance needs. Priced by contract, with a minimum of 25 seats.
Includes everything in Pro, plus single sign-on (SAML), audit logs, data residency in the EU,
a 99.9% uptime commitment and a named customer success manager. Enterprise support is
available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for high-priority tickets.

## Changing plan

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the
current billing period. Seats can be added at any time; removing seats on an annual plan
only reduces the bill at renewal.
