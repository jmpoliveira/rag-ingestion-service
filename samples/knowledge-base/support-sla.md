# Support response times (SLA)

These are the first-response targets of the Tessera support team. They apply to tickets
that customers open with us, not to the tickets they handle in their own workspace.

| Priority | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| High (service down, data at risk) | 8 business hours | 1 hour, around the clock |
| Medium (a feature does not work) | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| Low (questions, how-to) | 3 business days | 1 business day |

Business hours are 08:00 to 18:00 Lisbon time, Monday to Friday, excluding Portuguese public
holidays. Free workspaces have no response target and are answered through the community
forum.

## Escalation

If a high-priority ticket has no answer within its target, reply to the ticket with the word
ESCALATE. It is then routed to the duty manager. Enterprise customers can also call their
customer success manager directly.

## Service credits

When monthly uptime falls below the 99.9% commitment, Enterprise customers receive a
service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for each full 0.1% below the target, up to 50%.
Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
