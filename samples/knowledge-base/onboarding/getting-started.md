# Getting started with Tessera

Welcome to Tessera Support Cloud. These five steps take a new workspace from sign-up to its
first answered ticket, usually in under an hour.

## Step 1: choose your plan

Every workspace starts on the Free plan. You can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise at any time from
the billing page; see the Plans and pricing article to compare them.

## Step 2: invite your team

Invite your agents from the team page. Each agent who accepts takes one seat. Colleagues who
only need to follow tickets can be light agents, which are free on paid plans.

## Step 3: connect your support address

Forward your existing support email address to the address shown in the email channel
settings. From then on, every email to your support address becomes a ticket.

## Step 4: set up the basics

- Create a few macros for your most frequent questions.
- Create views for your main queues, for example by group or priority.
- If you promise response times to your own customers, create an SLA policy.

## Step 5: answer your first ticket

Send a test email to your support address, open the ticket in the "Unassigned" view, reply,
and mark it as resolved. The customer receives your reply by email.

Need help? Open a ticket with the Tessera support team from the help menu, or read the
Administrator Guide.
